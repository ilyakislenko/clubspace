## Кратко (для владельца)

Сейчас человек с улицы может только «Вступить» в клуб через приложение, а для этого нужен аккаунт. Записаться
на пробное ему некуда. Заявки с сайта, звонки и сообщения живут в блокноте, и никто не видит, сколько людей
дошло до занятия и сколько из них купили абонемент.

Что сделаем:
1. **Форма «Записаться на пробное»** на странице клуба `/c/<адрес>` и по QR-плакату.
   - Поля: имя, телефон, e-mail (необязательно), «для ребёнка» (имя и возраст), направление, комментарий.
   - Галочка согласия на обработку данных по 152-ФЗ, заранее не отмечена.
   - Оператором данных в согласии назван сам клуб. Его название и адрес клуб вписывает в настройках; без
     них форму не включить.
   - Защита от спама: лимит с одного адреса, скрытое поле-ловушка, дубли по телефону склеиваются.
2. **Вкладка «Ученики → Пробные»** для владельца, администраторов и главного тренера.
   - Воронка: новая → связались → записан на пробное → пришёл → купил / отказ (с причиной).
   - Заявку можно завести вручную, если человек позвонил или пришёл сам.
   - «Купил» ставится сам, когда у ученика из заявки появляется оплаченный абонемент.
3. **Из заявки в ученики в два клика.** «Добавить в клуб» открывает обычное приглашение с уже заполненными
   данными (взрослый или ребёнок с родителем), и заявка связывается с учеником.
4. **Напоминания.**
   - На «Сегодня» — строка «Заявки на пробное · N»: новые заявки и прошедшие пробные без отметки.
   - Push о каждой новой заявке и утром, если кто-то ждёт звонка больше суток.
   - Тренер группы получает «на пробное придёт Анна, пт 18:00», без телефона.
5. **«Отчёты → Пробные»**:
   - по источникам (страница клуба, QR, вручную): сколько заявок пришло, дошло до пробного и купило;
   - причины отказов;
   - как быстро клуб перезванивает.
6. **Данные не копятся.**
   - Через 90 дней после отказа или перехода в ученики имя, телефон и комментарии стираются, а цифры для
     отчёта остаются.
   - Заявка без движения 180 дней закрывается и стирается так же.
   - По просьбе человека «Удалить данные» стирает всё сразу.

«Заявки» (вступление, заморозки и т. д.) не меняются: там ученики ждут решения клуба, а в «Пробных» клуб сам
ведёт новых людей. Тренерам вкладка не видна: в ней чужие телефоны. Приложение для заявок в этой версии не
делаем, работа идёт в админке.

## Problem

1. **No way in without an account.** The public page offers only «Вступить» → `/join/<code>`
   (`apps/client/src/app/c/[slug]/page.tsx:41-48`). It opens the app and files a join request for a signed-in
   user (`join-request.service.ts` `requestMembership`). A parent wanting a trial class for a child has no
   form, and the club has no list.
2. **The building blocks exist, scattered:**
   - an unauthenticated form with consent, honeypot and an IP throttle, but for ClubSpace's own demo funnel
     (`demo-requests/create-demo-request.handler.ts:34-39`, 5/h per IP at `demo-requests.controller.ts:36-38`,
     `DemoRequest` at `schema.prisma:3194-3210`);
   - versioned consent with a server constant pinned to client editions (`AUTOPAY_CONSENT_VERSION`,
     `docs/legal/README.md:46-55`);
   - a server QR and a printable poster (`domains/workspace/join-qr.ts:1-7`,
     `admin/src/features/invite/invite-poster.ts`);
   - invite and «Добавить ребёнка» (`invite-workspace-member.feature.ts:4-11`,
     `add-workspace-dependent.feature.ts:9-17`);
   - free trial tariffs (`Plan.isFreeTrial`, `schema.prisma:2164`) and `normalizePhone`
     (`shared/utils/phone.ts:12`);
   - a nightly purge (`scheduler/data-purge.service.ts`) and a club-morning job
     (`daily-club.service.ts:22-26`).
3. **Legal gaps for strangers' data.**
   - `ConsentRecord` needs a `userId` (`schema.prisma:2953-2974`).
   - The club is the operator of its prospects' data, yet `Workspace` stores no operator name or address
     (`:93-274`).
   - The DPA's subjects stop at «приглашённые в Клуб или подавшие заявку на вступление»
     (`client/src/features/legal/dpa/dpa-2026-11-01.tsx:103-114`).
   - `docs/legal/README.md:204-210` already flags the demo form for lacking a consent text and a retention
     term; this design must not repeat that.

## Goal / non-goals

**Goal.** Every trial request (web, QR or phone) lands in one list. Staff move it to «пришёл» and turn it into
a member in two clicks. The owner sees conversion by source, and no phone number outlives its purpose.

**Non-goals.**
- Mobile UI for leads; SMS, WhatsApp or calling (E7).
- Real class bookings for leads (capacity, waitlist).
- Paid trials, a CAPTCHA provider, UTM tracking (sources are `page | qr | manual` only).
- Coaches and direction managers working leads.
- Any change to the requests inbox.

## 1. The lead rule — `domains/leads/lead-rules.ts` (single definition)

**Statuses** (stored):
- `NEW → CONTACTED → TRIAL_BOOKED → CAME`, plus `LOST` from any of them, with a reason.
- Staff may jump forward. The only way back is «Вернуть в работу» (LOST → CONTACTED).

**Bought** (derived; never stored or hand-set): the lead is linked (`convertedUserId`), and that user holds a
paid, non-free-trial abonement of this club with `amountPaid > 0`. The base filter is `paidInRangeWhere({})`
from `revenue.ts`, plus `plan.isFreeTrial: false`. The displayed status is `BOUGHT` when this holds, else the
stored status.

**Needs action** (the «Сегодня» count, default filter, tab badge): `NEW`, plus `TRIAL_BOOKED` with
`trialAt < now`.

**Open** = not LOST, not linked, not anonymised.

**Duplicate** = an open lead of the same club with the same normalised phone, created ≤ 30 days ago.

**Lost reasons** (sport-agnostic enum): `NO_ANSWER` «Не дозвонились» · `CHANGED_MIND` «Передумал» · `PRICE` «Не
подошла цена» · `SCHEDULE` «Не подошло расписание» · `LOCATION` «Далеко» · `CHOSE_OTHER` «Выбрал другой клуб» ·
`SPAM` «Спам» (anonymises at once, excluded from every report) · `OTHER` «Другое» (comment required).

**Retention** (constants, not env, because the consent text quotes them):
- `LEAD_CLOSED_RETENTION_DAYS = 90` after `lostAt` or `convertedAt`;
- `LEAD_IDLE_DAYS = 180` after `statusChangedAt` on an open lead. The lead is first closed as LOST
  `NO_ANSWER` («закрыта автоматически»).

**Anonymise** nulls `name, phone, email, childName, comment, staffNote, lostComment, consentIp,
consentUserAgent` and sets `anonymizedAt`. Source, statuses, dates, direction, reason and `convertedUserId`
stay, so reports keep counting.

## 2. Data model (Prisma, additive; `db push` without `--accept-data-loss`)

```prisma
enum LeadStatus { NEW CONTACTED TRIAL_BOOKED CAME LOST }
enum LeadSource { PUBLIC_PAGE QR MANUAL }
enum LeadLostReason { NO_ANSWER CHANGED_MIND PRICE SCHEDULE LOCATION CHOSE_OTHER SPAM OTHER }

model Lead {                         // «Пробные»: a person who asked for a trial; not an account
  id String @id @default(uuid()) @db.Uuid
  workspaceId String @db.Uuid        // → Workspace, Cascade
  source LeadSource   status LeadStatus @default(NEW)   statusChangedAt DateTime @default(now())
  name String? @db.VarChar(120)   phone String? @db.VarChar(20)   email String? @db.VarChar(254)  // null once anonymised
  forChild Boolean @default(false)   childName String? @db.VarChar(80)   childAge Int?
  directionId String? @db.Uuid /* → Direction, SetNull */   directionName String? @db.VarChar(255)
  comment String? @db.Text           // the person's own words
  staffNote String? @db.Text
  trialAt DateTime?   trialGroupId String? @db.Uuid /* → Group, SetNull */   trialGroupName String? @db.VarChar(255)
  contactedAt DateTime?   trialBookedAt DateTime?   cameAt DateTime?   lostAt DateTime?
  lostReason LeadLostReason?   lostComment String? @db.VarChar(500)
  convertedUserId String? @db.Uuid /* → User @relation("ConvertedLeads"), SetNull */   convertedAt DateTime?
  consentVersion String @db.VarChar(64)   consentAt DateTime
  consentIp String? @db.VarChar(64)   consentUserAgent String? @db.VarChar(512)
  consentRecordedById String? @db.Uuid   // manual entry: the staff member who attests it; no FK
  createdById String? @db.Uuid           // null = the public form
  resubmittedAt DateTime?   anonymizedAt DateTime?
  createdAt DateTime @default(now())   updatedAt DateTime @updatedAt
  @@index([workspaceId, status, createdAt])
  @@index([workspaceId, createdAt])
  @@index([workspaceId, phone])
  @@index([anonymizedAt, statusChangedAt])   // nightly retention pass
}
```

Fields are packed for reading; the schema has one per line.

`Workspace` gains:
- `trialRequestsEnabled Boolean @default(false)`;
- `pdOperatorName String? @db.VarChar(255)`;
- `pdOperatorAddress String? @db.VarChar(500)`.

`User` gains the back-relation. No backfill.

## 3. Server

### 3.1 Domain — `apps/server/src/domains/leads/`

- **`lead-rules.ts`**: §1 as pure functions and where-builders: `needsActionWhere(now)`,
  `boughtUserWhere(workspaceId)`, `duplicateWhere`, `displayStatus`, `retentionWhere(now)`, `anonymizeData`.
- **`lead-consent.ts`**: `LEAD_CONSENT_VERSION = '2026-12-01'` (the autopay pattern). Another version from the
  form → 400 «Текст согласия обновился — обновите страницу».
- **`lead-report.ts`** `loadLeadConversion(prisma, { workspaceId, from, to })`:
  - cohort = leads created in the range, SPAM excluded;
  - per source and total: `received`, `contacted` (reached CONTACTED or later), `trialBooked`, `came`,
    `bought`, `lostByReason`, and median hours from `createdAt` to `contactedAt`.
- **`lead-notifications.ts`**: recipients are the owner plus ACTIVE members whose role is in `SPORT_LEADS`.

### 3.2 Public endpoints — `features/public-trial-requests/` (controller `public/clubs/:slug/trial`, no guards)

| Endpoint | Method name | Throttle | DTOs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GET public/clubs/:slug/trial` | `getPublicTrialForm` | 120/min/IP (the `PUBLIC_THROTTLE` values, `public-clubs.controller.ts:35-37`; export the constant) | → `PublicTrialFormOutput { clubName, logoUrl, directions[{id,name}], operator{name,address}, consentVersion, contacts }` |
| `POST public/clubs/:slug/trial` | `createPublicTrialRequest` | `TRIAL_REQUEST_THROTTLE` 10/h per `trackByClientIp` | `CreatePublicTrialRequestInput` → `CreatePublicTrialRequestOutput { status: 'received' }` |

**Lookup.**
- The slug resolves through `WorkspacePublicSlug`. `isListed` is not required: a club may take requests
  without appearing in search.
- 404 «Клуб не принимает заявки» unless `isActive`, `trialRequestsEnabled`, `pdOperatorName` and
  `pdOperatorAddress` all hold.

**Input** (one flat `z.object` + `superRefine`):
- `name` 1–120;
- `phone` → `normalizePhone`, else 400 «Проверьте номер телефона»;
- `email?`;
- `forChild`, with `childName` 1–80 and `childAge` 1–17 when it is set;
- `directionId?` (ignored unless it is the club's); `comment?` ≤ 1000;
- `source: page|qr`; `consent: z.literal(true)`; `consentVersion`;
- `website?` (honeypot).

**Handler order:**
1. The honeypot is filled → `received`, store nothing (`create-demo-request.handler.ts:34-36`).
2. Consent and version checks.
3. The club cap: more than 50 public leads in 24 h → 429 «Сейчас заявку принять не получится — позвоните в
   клуб», logged.
4. Duplicate → append «{date}: {comment}» to `comment` and set `resubmittedAt`. No new row, no second push.
5. Otherwise create with `consentAt = now`, the client IP and the user agent.
6. Audit `lead.create {source}` (actor null), realtime `leads`, notify `lead.created`.

The answer is always `received`, so a bot can't tell a duplicate or a trap from success.

### 3.3 Staff endpoints — `features/leads/` (controller `workspaces/:workspaceId/leads`)

| Endpoint | Method name | Tier (`@Roles` + handler) | DTOs | Audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GET leads?view=&source=&q=&curPage=&perPage=` | `listLeads` | `SPORT_LEADS` + `assertSportLead` | `ListLeadsQuery` → `ListLeadsOutput { data, total, counts{needsAction,new,trialOverdue,contacted,trialBooked,came,bought,lost} }` | — |
| `GET leads/:leadId` | `getLead` | `SPORT_LEADS` | → `LeadOutput` (+ `updatedAt`) | — |
| `POST leads` | `createLead` | `SPORT_LEADS` | `CreateLeadInput` (public fields + `consentConfirmed: true`) → `LeadOutput` | `lead.create` |
| `PATCH leads/:leadId` | `updateLead` | `SPORT_LEADS` | `UpdateLeadInput` (contact, direction, `staffNote`, `...ExpectedUpdatedAtField`) | `lead.update` {fields} |
| `POST leads/:leadId/status` | `setLeadStatus` | `SPORT_LEADS` | `SetLeadStatusInput { from, to, trialAt?, trialGroupId?, notifyCoach?, lostReason?, lostComment? }` | `lead.setStatus` {from,to,reason} |
| `POST leads/:leadId/link` | `linkLeadMember` | `SPORT_LEADS` | `LinkLeadMemberInput { userId }` | `lead.link` {userId} |
| `POST leads/:leadId/erase` | `eraseLead` | `SPORT_LEADS` | → `LeadOutput` | `lead.erase` |
| `GET leads/report?from=&to=` | `getLeadConversionReport` | `SPORT_LEADS` | → `LeadConversionReportOutput` | — |
| `GET leads/trial-link` | `getTrialLink` | `SPORT_LEADS` | → `TrialLinkOutput { url, qrPngDataUrl, missing: disabled\|slug\|operator\|null }` | — |
| `GET exports/leads.csv?from=&to=` | `leads` (export controller) | `CLUB_ADMINS` | CSV | `export.leads` |

**`listLeads`.**
- `view`: `needsAction` (default) · `new` · `contacted` · `trialBooked` · `came` (not bought) · `bought`
  (`boughtUserWhere`) · `lost` · `all`.
- Order: needs action oldest first, other views by `statusChangedAt desc`.
- `q` searches name and phone digits.
- Anonymised rows show only under `lost` and `all`, as «Данные удалены».

**`createLead`.** A duplicate → 409 `{ «Заявка с этим телефоном уже есть», leadId }`. The handler records
`consentVersion = LEAD_CONSENT_VERSION` and `consentRecordedById` = the invoker.

**`setLeadStatus`.**
- Compare-and-set on `from` (`updateMany … status: from`); 0 rows → 409 «Статус заявки изменился — обновите
  экран» (the inbox pattern).
- `TRIAL_BOOKED` needs `trialAt`. `trialGroupId` is optional and must be the club's; its name is
  denormalised.
- `LOST` needs a reason, plus a comment for `OTHER`. `SPAM` anonymises in the same transaction.
- Every move stamps its `*At` and `statusChangedAt`.
- An anonymised lead → 400 «Данные заявки удалены».

**`linkLeadMember`.**
- `userId` must be an ACTIVE `user`-role member of the club (a child counts), else 400.
- The same user again is idempotent; a different user → 409 «Заявка уже связана с другим учеником».
- Sets `convertedUserId` and `convertedAt`, and moves earlier statuses to `CAME`.

**`eraseLead`** anonymises now: a 152-ФЗ ст. 21 request.

**Audit metadata never holds names, phones, e-mails or comments.** The audit log outlives lead retention.

**Settings.** `UpdateClubPublicProfileFeatureInputSchema` (`update-club-public-profile.feature.ts:20-34`,
`CLUB_ADMINS`) gains `trialRequestsEnabled`, `pdOperatorName` and `pdOperatorAddress` (`optionalText`).
Turning the form on is refused with 400:
- without both operator fields: «Укажите оператора персональных данных — название и адрес»;
- while the club's latest `ClubLegalAcceptance.dpaVersion` is older than `LEADS_DPA_MIN_VERSION =
  '2026-12-01'`: «Сначала владелец принимает новую редакцию поручения на обработку данных («Подписка
  ClubSpace»)» (§8).

## 4. Admin UI

### 4.1 Where it lives — «Ученики → Пробные» (`users/trials`)

`memberTabs` (`sections.ts:11-24`) gains a third tab: `{ key: "users-trials", label: "Пробные", path:
"users/trials", access: "sportLead" }`. `layout.tsx` wraps it in `SportLeadGate`, and the tab badge is
`counts.needsAction`. The sidebar keeps 11 items.

**Why a tab and not a fifth kind in «Заявки».** «Заявки» is what a member asked for, waiting on one staff
decision, with nothing read-only (`requests-inbox.md:137-141`). A lead differs on every axis:
- it is not a member and has no account;
- it is a multi-step pipeline over days, not one decision;
- different viewers see it: sport leads only, because of strangers' phones;
- it has its own retention.

Mixing them would leave inbox rows sitting for weeks in a coach-visible list that would have to hide phones.
So each job gets one place: «ответить ученикам» → «Заявки», «довести нового человека до абонемента» →
«Пробные». The conversion report sits with the other reports, in «Отчёты → Пробные» (`analytics/trials`,
`access: "sportLead"`).

### 4.3 Panel — `features/leads/LeadSheet.tsx`

**Facts:**
- phone (`tel:` link + copy), e-mail, child, direction, comment;
- source and «получена 2 ч назад», plus «повторная заявка {дата}» after a merge;
- consent «согласие {дата}, редакция {v}» or «согласие подтвердил(а) {сотрудник}»;
- a timeline from the `*At` columns.

**«Заметка»** (`staffNote`): dirty guard and `expectedUpdatedAt`; its «Сохранить» is an outline button, so
the filled button stays the next step.

One filled button per state, an outline secondary, and «⋯» with «Отказ…», «Изменить данные», «Удалить
данные»:

| State | Filled | Outline |
|---|---|---|
| NEW | «Связались» | «Записать на пробное» |
| CONTACTED | «Записать на пробное» | — |

Each form swaps the panel body (no panel on a panel):

- **«Отказ…»**: a reason radio list and «Комментарий» (required for «Другое»), then «Отметить отказ»

## 12. Decisions

- **«Ученики → Пробные» as its own tab, not a kind in «Заявки»** (§4.1): the subject, lifecycle, viewers and
  retention all differ. The report lives in «Отчёты → Пробные». No new sidebar item.
- **Viewers = sport leads** (owner, admin, head coach), the roster tier. A coach gets only a name-and-time
  heads-up for their own group. Only club admins export.
- **«Купил» is derived** from the linked member's paid, non-free-trial abonement. Nobody can tick it by hand,
  so the report stays honest.
- **Conversion reuses the invite and add-child forms** (prefill + link). There is no `convertLead` endpoint
  duplicating invite logic.
- **The club is the operator.**
  - The public form needs the operator's name and address, plus the owner's acceptance of the DPA edition
    that lists lead subjects.
  - Manual entry needs the staff member to confirm verbal consent; it is recorded with their id.
- **Consent lives on the lead row** (version, time, IP, UA), because `ConsentRecord` needs an account.
  Versioning follows the autopay pattern.
- **Retention**: 90 days after closing, 180 days idle, immediately on request or spam. These are constants,
  because the consent text quotes them. Reports survive anonymisation, and the audit holds no PD.
- **Spam protection**:
  - 10/h per IP, not the demo form's 5: Russian mobile carriers put many users behind one IP;
  - the honeypot, the duplicate merge and a cap of 50 a day per club;
  - no CAPTCHA, since a third-party processor would need a DPA entry.
- **The form works for unlisted clubs** (noindex). Search listing stays a separate choice.
- **Reminders**: the «Сегодня» row, a push per new lead, one morning `lead.stale` per club-day, and the coach
  heads-up.
- **Fixed statuses** (five stored plus the derived «Купил») and fixed lost reasons with «Другое».
- **AI gets counts only.**
- **(implementation, S3) The club page learns «form on» from `GET public/clubs/:slug/trial`** (404 = off),
  fetched next to the club; `PublicClubOutput` stays unchanged. The trial page reads `isListed` from the same
  answer for noindex and the «← Страница клуба» link.
- **(implementation, S3) The form sends the edition its consent page shows**: the server's
  `consentVersion` when the client registry has that text, else the newest client edition (the server then
  answers «Текст согласия обновился — обновите страницу»). Field limits come from the generated request
  schema (`trial-limits.ts`), never retyped.
- **(implementation, S3) The consent link opens in a new tab**, so reading the text doesn't lose a filled
  form. `/c/<slug>/trial/consent` is always noindex and 404s with the form; a trial-route `not-found.tsx`
  says «Клуб не принимает заявки».
- **(implementation, S3) DPA 2026-12-01 also names the purpose** (раздел 2: приём и учёт заявок на пробное)
  and p. 1.1, not only §3/§4: a subject without a purpose would leave the processing unexplained.
- **(implementation, S3) The client spec pins three server constants**: the newest lead-consent edition =
  `LEAD_CONSENT_VERSION`, the 90/180 days in the text = `LEAD_CLOSED_RETENTION_DAYS`/`LEAD_IDLE_DAYS`, and
  `DPA_EDITIONS` holds `LEADS_DPA_MIN_VERSION`.
- **(implementation, S3) Mobile**: `lead.created`, `lead.stale`, `lead.trialBooked` route to `null` explicitly,
  so a stray `videoId` in their metadata can never open a video.
- **(implementation, S2) «Купил» reads without a date** («купил абонемент» in the row, an undated last step in
  the timeline): the lead stores no purchase date, and the panel does not fetch the member's payments for it.
- **(implementation, S2) The poster heading follows the club's words** («Пробная тренировка» / «Пробное
  занятие», `agree(session, …)`), not a fixed «Пробное занятие»: the admin's vocabulary guard forbids the
  hard-coded noun.
- **(implementation, S2) «Добавить в клуб» for an existing account that must accept the invite** (`outcome:
  invited`) does not call `linkLeadMember` (there is no active member yet); the panel says to link with
  «Связать с учеником» once accepted.
- **(implementation, S2) The panel loads its lead itself** (`getLead`, reloaded on `leads`), so `?lead=` opens
  any lead — another view, a later page, or the duplicate a manual entry pointed at.
- **(implementation, S2) The settings block also offers «QR-плакат»** next to «Открыть форму» (the task asked
  for the poster in the card); the pipeline's «⋯» keeps the same item for sport leads who can't open settings.
  The switch is disabled until the owner accepted DPA 2026-12-01 (a notice links «Подписка ClubSpace»); a server
  refusal of the switch shows inside the block.
- **(implementation, S2) Rows highlight what «Нужно действие» counts** using the server's `needsAction` flag,
  never a client re-derivation. Lead audit metadata uses distinct keys (`leadSource`, `fromStatus`, `toStatus`,
  `lostReason`, `fields` with `personName`) so the global audit labels don't collide with dates or autopay.
- **(implementation, S1) No personal data of a lead in any notification** (Main's decision: Expo/web push is
  a cross-border transfer, and the inbox row is the push). `lead.created` = «Новая заявка на пробное» /
  «{направление}. Откройте «Ученики → Пробные».»; `lead.stale` = «Заявки ждут звонка» / «Заявки на пробное
  ждут ответа больше суток: N»; `lead.trialBooked` = «На пробное придёт новый человек» / «сб, 12 дек.,
  18:00 · «группа»» — when and where, not who (no name, no child's age). Kinds `lead.*` are mute-exempt (staff
  work); dispatch keys: `lead.stale` 7 days, `lead.created`/`lead.trialBooked` 30.
- **(implementation, S1) Idle leads are closed and anonymised in the same nightly pass** (LOST `NO_ANSWER`,
  `anonymizedAt` now), not 90 days after the close: the consent promises at most 180 days without activity.
- **(implementation, S1) The public `consent` is `z.boolean()`, checked in the handler after the honeypot**
  (not `z.literal(true)`), so a trap-filling bot gets «received» rather than a 400.
- **(implementation, S1, reversed after review) A duplicate merge keeps the FIRST consent evidence** (edition,
  time, IP, UA): anyone who knows the phone can resubmit, and a staff-entered lead keeps its staff consent;
  `resubmittedAt` records the repeat. The earlier S1 choice to refresh the evidence on a merge is withdrawn.
  The appended comment stops growing at 4 000 characters (`resubmittedAt` is still set).
- **(implementation, S1) Moves:** forward jumps, `TRIAL_BOOKED → TRIAL_BOOKED` reschedules (new `trialAt`, a
  new coach heads-up), LOST → CONTACTED clears the refusal; any other move is 400 «Так перевести заявку
  нельзя». `trialAt` is a wall clock in the group's gym zone, else the club's; `LeadOutput.trialTimezone`
  names that zone, and the panel, reschedule prefill and CSV show the trial in it. Linking moves any earlier
  status, a refusal included, to CAME (joining means they came) and is compare-and-set on
  `convertedUserId IS NULL`. `contactedAt` is stamped once — by the first move past NEW or by linking — and
  «Вернуть в работу» never moves it, so «перезваниваем через…» measures the first reaction.
  Every write except erase refuses an anonymised lead (400); erase is idempotent.
- **(implementation, S1) Views:** stored-status views exclude bought leads (BOUGHT wins on display); `bought`
  and `came` exclude anonymised rows, `lost`/`all` show them. `ListLeadsOutput` adds `formEnabled` and
  `timezone`; `LeadOutput` adds `displayStatus`, `needsAction`, `consentRecordedByName`, `convertedUserName`,
  `timezone`. Manual entry pushes the other sport leads, never the creator.
- **(implementation, S1) Profile fields are optional on update** (omitted = unchanged), so older clients keep
  saving; the DPA gate applies only when switching the form on, the operator rule whenever it is on. Output adds
  `trialFormUrl` and `leadsDpaAccepted`.
- **(implementation, S1) `PLATFORM_DPA_VERSION` keeps its 2026-11-01 default** (Main's call): the demo clubs'
  acceptance follows the env, and their trial form is on only when that edition lists lead subjects — run the
  demo with `PLATFORM_DPA_VERSION=2026-12-01` to show `/c/<slug>/trial`. AI gets `club_overview.leads`
  (counts) for the whole-club scope only.

## 14. Open risks

- **The legal texts are drafts** (`docs/legal/README.md:3-7`). The lawyer should confirm:
  - whether a checkbox naming the operator meets ст. 9 for web forms, or whether ч. 1 ст. 6 п. 5
    (pre-contract steps at the person's request) already suffices;
  - whether verbal consent recorded by staff is enough evidence;
  - the parent clause, given the open 14/16 age question (`README.md:139-143`).
- **The DPA re-acceptance gate** blocks the public form in every club until its owner accepts 2026-12-01.
  That is intended, and the banner already exists.
- **CGNAT**: 10/h per IP may still block a gym's wifi on an open day. The club cap and the duplicate merge are
  the real guard. Keep the throttle constant exported so it can be raised.
- **«Купил» needs a link.** Someone who joins alone through the app code stays «пришёл» until staff link
  them, and the report undercounts until then.
- **No mobile UI.** Owners of small clubs who live on the phone get pushes but must open the admin. The first
  v2 candidate is a read-and-call list in the app for sport leads.
- **Head coaches see phones** in the list, which matches their roster access today.
